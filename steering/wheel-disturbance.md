# Wheel vibration and tire pull

These are separate complaints. Use [wheel vibration](../sources/passages/wheel-vibration.md)
for repeatable speed-related shake and [tire pull](../sources/passages/tire-pull.md)
for sustained directional effort. Neither passage establishes a customer repair.

| Observation | Still unresolved | Next useful distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Balance display accepts the assembly | Loaded tire variation, runout, bead seating | Separate loaded-force and runout measurements |
| Balance changes after remounting | Setup repeatability | Centering/adaptor verification before adding weights |
| Pull changes after a permitted tire-position change | Which tire mechanism; other concurrent faults | Position record and lateral-force investigation |
| Pull occurs primarily during braking | Tire/alignment explanation may be incomplete | Brake-drag/force and mechanical evidence |
| Vibration follows road speed | Wheel assembly versus driveline/suspension | Correlate complaint with independent component evidence |

The last two rows are diagnostic inferences, not claims that Hunter's product page
diagnosed those faults. Existing [front-end patterns](../notes/front-end-diagnostic-patterns.md)
and [alignment integrity](../sources/passages/alignment-integrity.md) remain
applicable. Steering-wheel versus seat location is a clue, not component proof.

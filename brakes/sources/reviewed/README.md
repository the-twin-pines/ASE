# Slow source-review pass

This directory is for the slower pass over the supplied brake videos. It is deliberately separate from the title/metadata indexes under `sources/notes/` and `sources/brakes-ii/notes/`.

## Evidence states

- **indexed** — playlist title/channel/duration/URL only.
- **partial source review** — some source-level content is inspectable (for example indexed chapter timestamps or a companion document), but the full video transcript/captions have not been read.
- **transcript reviewed** — the full available transcript/captions have been read closely.
- **corroborated** — a technical claim has also been checked against a service manual, manufacturer document, or comparably strong source.

Do not promote a claim merely because a video title suggests it. Keep application-specific numbers and procedures labeled as such rather than turning them into universal ASE rules.

## Current slow-pass focus

The first coherent source family is The Ram Man Inc. material because the supplied brake playlists contain a substantial cluster of its diagnostic, hydraulic, proportioning/metering, master-cylinder, booster, and pedal-travel videos. The Ram Man also publishes companion brake-hydraulic/service PDFs, which makes it possible to distinguish what their material actually says from generic brake lore.

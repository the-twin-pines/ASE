# Gaskets, assembly and rebuild sequence

Source grouping is based on titles/metadata only.

## General study notes — not extracted claims from the videos

- Gasket sealing depends on surface condition, flatness, correct gasket type/orientation, fastener condition and the specified tightening procedure.
- Head-gasket diagnosis should distinguish combustion leakage, coolant leakage, oil leakage and loss of clamping/sealing from unrelated overheating causes.
- Engine assembly is a sequence of measurements and checks, not only a parts-installation sequence. Critical clearances should be established before final closure.
- Cleanliness matters especially at bearings, oil passages, cylinder walls, ring lands and sealing surfaces.
- Assembly lubricant, engine oil and sealants have different jobs; use the specified material in the specified location.
- Exterior paint/finish is useful preservation and identification work, but it is separate from internal mechanical acceptance.

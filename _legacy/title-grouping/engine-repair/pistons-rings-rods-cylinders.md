# Pistons, rings, rods and cylinders

Source grouping is based on titles/metadata only.

## General study notes — not extracted claims from the videos

- Piston/rod assemblies are directional on many engines: piston marks, valve reliefs, rod chamfers, bearing tangs and cylinder-bank orientation may matter.
- Ring installation requires correct ring identity, orientation, end gap and clocking according to the ring/engine instructions.
- Ring end gap is checked in the actual bore; bore diameter and application affect the acceptable gap.
- A ring compressor must bring the rings into the bore without catching or chipping them; resistance during installation is a reason to stop and inspect.
- Press-fit piston pins require controlled support and temperature/press technique so the pin, piston and rod are not distorted.
- Piston-to-wall clearance, ring side clearance and bearing clearances are different measurements with different failure modes.

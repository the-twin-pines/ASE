# ABS, wheel-speed sensing, hydraulic modulation, stability control

Indexing pass over the supplied brake playlist. Grouping is based on titles/metadata; video contents have not yet been transcript-checked.

## Study spine

- Follow the chain: wheel motion → tone target/encoder → sensor → wiring → module input → control decision → valves/pump → wheel pressure.
- Passive and active wheel-speed sensors have different electrical behavior; test strategy must match sensor type.
- Sensor air gap, rust/debris, damaged tone rings or magnetic encoders, wiring faults and wheel-bearing movement can all corrupt speed signals.
- An ABS warning can come from bad input, module/power faults, or a hydraulic actuator fault; do not jump directly to the pump.
- ABS bleeding may require commanding valves/pump with a scan tool after the ordinary hydraulic circuit is bled.
- Traction and stability control reuse wheel-speed information and brake-pressure control, so one bad wheel signal can create symptoms beyond the ABS lamp.

# Boosters, proportioning/metering valves, warning and stop-light switches

Indexing pass over the supplied brake playlist. Grouping is based on titles/metadata; video contents have not yet been transcript-checked.

## Study spine

- Brake assist changes pedal effort; it does not create hydraulic fluid volume or fix excessive clearance.
- For a vacuum booster, exhausting stored vacuum and then starting the engine while holding pedal force tests whether assist arrives.
- Hydroboost uses hydraulic pressure rather than manifold vacuum; diagnosis follows its pressure supply and accumulator path.
- Proportioning, metering, residual and combination valves have different purposes; identify the actual valve before assigning a symptom.
- A pressure-differential/shuttle switch can illuminate a warning lamp when the two hydraulic circuits no longer behave alike.
- Stop-light switch adjustment/electrical faults can affect brake lamps and, on some vehicles, interlocks or control-module logic without being a hydraulic brake fault.

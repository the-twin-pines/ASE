# Disc brakes: calipers, pads, hardware, lubrication

Indexing pass over the supplied brake playlist. Grouping is based on playlist metadata/titles; these entries are **not** being treated as transcripts or as verified claims from the videos yet.

## Study spine

- Separate piston binding, slide-pin binding, pad-to-bracket binding, trapped hydraulic pressure, and parking-brake mechanism faults.
- A hot wheel is evidence of drag, not by itself a diagnosis of which component is holding the brake.
- Pad wear patterns are evidence about relative motion; interpret inboard/outboard wear in the context of the caliper design.
- Lubricants must be compatible with rubber boots/bushings and kept off friction surfaces.
- Hardware, shims, abutment clips, and anti-rattle springs control pad location and noise; missing or corroded hardware can change both.
- When deciding rebuild versus replace, inspect piston surface, bore, seals/boots, bleeder condition, bracket and slide bores.

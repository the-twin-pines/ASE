# Rotors, runout, thickness variation, bedding, noise and pulsation

## Study spine

- Keep lateral runout, disc thickness variation and friction-material transfer/surface variation distinct.
- Hub cleanliness and bearing condition matter when measuring runout; a good rotor mounted on a dirty or moving hub can still wobble.
- Runout can contribute to pad knockback and can produce thickness variation over time.
- Pedal pulsation and steering-wheel shake are symptoms to localize, not automatic proof that a rotor is “warped.”
- Bedding/burnishing establishes a stable friction interface; procedure depends on pad/rotor manufacturer and vehicle use.
- Noise diagnosis should separate friction squeal, hardware rattle, dust-shield contact, bearing noise and periodic rotor-related contact.

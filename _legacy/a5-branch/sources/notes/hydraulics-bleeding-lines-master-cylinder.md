## Study spine

- A hydraulic brake system should transmit pressure with very little compressibility; air or vapor increases pedal travel.
- A pedal that slowly sinks under steady force suggests pressure is not being held; separate internal master-cylinder bypass from external leakage.
- A flexible hose can fail as a restriction: weak application if flow in is limited, or drag if pressure is trapped on release.
- Bleeding method and sequence depend on the hydraulic layout and whether the ABS modulator has trapped air.
- Brake line repairs need the correct tube, flare, union and routing; ordinary compression fittings are not a substitute for approved brake-line joints.
- Bench bleeding a master cylinder is about removing air from the cylinder before the vehicle circuit is expected to do it.

# Drum brakes and parking brakes

## Study spine

- Drum brakes depend on shoe orientation, springs, anchors, adjusters, wheel cylinders and correct drum diameter/surface.
- Excess shoe-to-drum clearance can create a low pedal that pumps up; adjustment is therefore part of pedal-travel diagnosis.
- Self-adjusters must be assembled on the correct side and able to move; reversed or frozen hardware defeats the mechanism.
- Parking-brake faults can create drag even when the service hydraulic circuit is healthy.
- Electronic parking-brake calipers add motors/gearing and service-mode requirements; do not assume a conventional piston can simply be pushed back.
- Machining decisions depend on diameter/thickness limits and surface condition, not merely whether a lathe can cut the part.

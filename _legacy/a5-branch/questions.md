# Recovered/current brake study threads

## Long pedal that improves on the second pump

- Does pump-up point toward assist, or toward clearance/compressibility?
- How do air/vapor, drum clearance, pad knockback, bearing/hub motion, leaks, and the master cylinder differ?
- What static tests separate them?

## One very hot wheel

- Is the brake mechanically stuck or is hydraulic pressure trapped?
- Does opening the bleeder release it?
- If pressure is trapped at one corner, is the local flexible hose acting as a restriction?
- If no pressure is trapped, are piston/slides/pads/parking-brake hardware binding?

## Heat followed by longer pedal

Can the drag fault itself generate enough heat to create a second hydraulic symptom?

Yes in principle: local overheating can heat the fluid enough to create vapor/compressibility. Treat that as a testable hypothesis, not an automatic conclusion.

## Booster vs hydraulic problem

Why isn't a booster test the same thing as a long-pedal test?

Because assist mainly changes required pedal force. Long travel/pump-up directs attention to hydraulic/mechanical travel and compressibility. Test both systems, but do not conflate them.

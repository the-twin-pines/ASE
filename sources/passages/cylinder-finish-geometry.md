# Crosshatch versus cylinder geometry

Source: `youtube-X3Crh_URvrk`, full stored caption text; visuals unreviewed.
The narrator separates surface contact patterns from later diameter/taper checks.

Inference: a fresh-looking finish does not establish bore size, taper, roundness
or compatible piston/ring fit. Measure the relevant geometry under the applicable
procedure; a hand-held hone or a photograph is not the measuring instrument.
Surface finishing and dimensional correction remain separate tasks. No universal
ban on a finishing tool, gasoline lubricant or wear allowance is adopted.

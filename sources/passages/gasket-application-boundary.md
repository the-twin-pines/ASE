# Head gasket: application before reuse or retorque advice

Sources: `youtube-wSn2YGY4rCY` and `felpro-gasket-technologies`.
The video compares gasket types but makes sweeping reuse/retorque claims.
Fel-Pro describes application-specific technologies and a named no-retorque family.

Inference: identify the exact gasket, fasteners and sealing surfaces before deciding
assembly treatment. A product-family property is not a rule for all modern engines.
The video's reuse, sealer and coolant-history claims remain unapproved; the reviewed
Fel-Pro page does not resolve that reuse question. Exact surface requirements,
bolt replacement, torque and any retorque instruction require the applicable
service/product documentation.

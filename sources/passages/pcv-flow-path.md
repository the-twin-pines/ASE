# Crankcase ventilation has an inlet and outlet

Source: `youtube-sAHuBJ6tOUc`, full stored caption text; visuals unreviewed.
The discussion contrasts road-draft ventilation with fresh-air entry and
manifold-vacuum extraction of crankcase vapors.

Inference: identify the installed ventilation design, then inspect its intended
flow path before attributing sludge, oil leakage or pressure to worn rings.
Restriction, leakage, wrong components and engine condition remain alternatives.
A generic valve-rattle check cannot certify every design. Historical mileage and
horsepower claims are not adopted; no universal crankcase pressure limit or
emissions-system modification is approved.

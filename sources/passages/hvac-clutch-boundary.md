# A/C request, relay output, clutch click and cooling

Historical source: `a7-field-notes-b5e9fd3`, Car Wizard shorter captioned cut and
bench demonstrations recorded in the pinned note. The full YouTube cut remains
unreviewed.

In that conventional-clutch demonstration, controlled relay substitution engaged
the clutch. Bench examples separated a failed coil from mechanical compressor
seizure. A click showed movement, not useful pumping. The pulley and compressor
hub were different objects to inspect.

Inference: ask which boundary the observation establishes. An A/C button lamp
cannot clear the complete command circuit; a moving clutch cannot clear the
refrigeration system. A compressor that turns can still have an electrical fault
or fail to pump.

First identify whether the vehicle actually has this relay/clutch arrangement.
For variable-displacement or electric compressors use architecture-specific
command and response evidence. No jumper terminal identification, permanent
bypass, high-voltage access or service-port venting procedure is admitted.

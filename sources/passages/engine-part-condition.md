# Reusing piston and rod parts

Source: `youtube-mB9ta-By88M`, full stored caption text; visuals unreviewed.
The narrator screens cracks, heat damage, pin condition and ring-groove damage.

Inference: preserve the failed component's identity, then distinguish obvious
damage from dimensions/material condition needing measurement. A visually clean
part or easy-moving cap is not a reuse approval. Teardown resistance is a clue,
not a calibrated fastener test. Follow exact component inspection and reuse rules;
no bore allowance, bolt tolerance or blanket reuse decision is supplied.

# Variable-displacement compressor: command versus output

Source: `denso-ac-service-2023`. Reviewed guidance calls for temperatures and live
data together with pressure on electronically controlled variable-displacement
systems.

Synthetic inference: a turning pulley with weak cooling leaves control,
refrigerant inventory, heat rejection and compressor output unresolved. Record
architecture, operating conditions, relevant command and permission data, and
pressure/temperature response during the fault. A displayed command does not
prove that the actuator received it or that pumping followed.

Choose the next test at the first unproved boundary using vehicle service
information. Do not require a clutch click on a clutchless system or prescribe
replacement from pressure alone. A high-voltage compressor needs its own approved
procedure. No command threshold, normal pressure or electrical bypass is supplied.

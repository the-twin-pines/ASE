# Tire pull after alignment

Source: `hunter-road-force-2026`. The reviewed page treats lateral tire force and
tire placement as a separate pull measurement.

Synthetic inference: pulling after alignment leaves tire force, road conditions,
brake drag, mechanical binding and valid alignment geometry as competing causes.
Preserve tire position and direction of pull. Only where fitment and rotation
rules permit, compare an appropriate tire-position change under repeatable
conditions. A changed pull supports a tire contribution; it does not certify all
other causes absent. A lateral-force measurement can investigate further.

Do not defeat directional or staggered-fitment requirements. Do not change caster
to compensate for an unlocalized pull. Apply the A4 measurement gate before
using alignment values. No universal tire force or alignment threshold is approved.

# Static A/C pressure versus charge mass

Historical source: `a7-field-notes-b5e9fd3`, Car Wizard refrigerant discussion.
The note distinguishes the presence of pressure from recovered refrigerant mass.
The recorded source used a recovery machine to quantify refrigerant. Its
service-port release shortcut is not a diagnostic procedure endorsed here.

Inference: “some pressure exists” and “the specified charge is present” answer
different questions. Warm vents and a low-side gauge alone cannot establish how
much refrigerant is missing, whether adding refrigerant is justified or why charge
was lost. Preserve service history and leakage evidence alongside measurements.

Identify refrigerant and exact vehicle charge specification before service.
Unknown charge/oil inventory remains UNKNOWN. Recovery, evacuation, leak testing
and charging require the applicable equipment and service procedure. No pressure
target, charge amount, oil quantity or top-up instruction is approved.

# A running car can still have a fuel-delivery fault

Source: `youtube-vnkm0Dvn_Ys`, full stored short's captions.
The presenter reports a damaged coil and argues that driving excludes a bad pump.
That exclusion is unsupported.

Inference: an engine running establishes delivery sufficient for that observed
condition, not every load or intermittent condition. Coil damage is a lead;
appropriate ignition, fuel and mechanical evidence must discriminate the fault.
The short lacks a documented swap test or post-repair trace. Do not adopt its title
as proof that replacing the coil solved the customer complaint.

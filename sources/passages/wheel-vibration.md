# Highway wheel vibration after balancing

Source: `hunter-road-force-2026`. The reviewed equipment page separates ordinary
balance from loaded tire uniformity, rim runout, bead seating and centering.

Synthetic inference: a “balanced” wheel does not exclude those other disturbance
mechanisms. Preserve wheel identity and complaint conditions; inspect tire/wheel
condition and measurement setup. If balance results change on remounting, settle
centering repeatability before treating the weights as reliable. If repeatable
balance leaves vibration, request separately recorded loaded-force/runout results.

Positive findings guide further diagnosis, not automatic tire replacement.
Road-speed association alone does not eliminate driveline or suspension causes.
Validate application-specific limits. A4 mechanical inspection and measurement
gates still apply; no alignment adjustment follows from vibration alone.

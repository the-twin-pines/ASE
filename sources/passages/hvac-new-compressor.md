# New A/C compressor does not prove a working compressor

Historical source: `a7-field-notes-b5e9fd3`, Watch Wes Work case in the pinned A7
note. This is a review of that note, not a new video replay.

The recorded Ram complaint arrived labeled as wiring trouble after compressor
replacement. Request data was true, the feed powered a test light, and inspection
found a clutch stuck engaged. Despite external drive, no pressure rise was observed.
Debris was suspected; teardown, repair and post-repair cooling were not shown.

Diagnostic lesson: keep request, usable output, clutch response, mechanical drive
and compressor pumping separate. A new component remains a candidate until its
behavior is tested. Circuit-current faults can coexist with an abnormal load.

Inference for a comparable complaint: verify architecture and reproduce the fault;
observe the next unproved boundary before selecting wiring or compressor repair.
A test light establishes only the load it actually applies, not every required
operating load. No direct-power procedure or current limit is supplied here.

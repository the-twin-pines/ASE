# Individually inspected steering sources

Review date: 2026-10-08. Reviewer: Codex, source inspection and explicitly labeled
engineering inferences. These are scoped paraphrases, not copied transcripts.
Each ID below has a separate registry receipt. The receipt is a review attestation,
not cryptographic proof that a person or model read the source. Someone who can

## trw-1250-2002

[TRW Steering Diagnostics Service Manual](https://www.trwaftermarket.com/globalassets/na/trucksteering---literature/other-manuals/other-manuals-sm/trw1250.pdf),
© 2002. Inspected notice/introduction, complaint charts and tests 3, 5, 7–10,
17–19, 21. Scope: commercial steering components, professional mechanics. The
notice requires vehicle/component service manuals and proper equipment. Flow,
pressure, leakage, mechanical isolation and alignment are separate tests. It does
not supply a passenger-car specification or a casual owner pressure-test procedure.
Text extraction exposes chart labels; no claim of visually verifying every arrow.

## ford-ssm-47522

[Ford SSM 47522](https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/tsbs/2018/MC-10149761-9999.pdf),
full one-page notice inspected. Scope: 2018 E-350/E-450 built 23–24 July 2018.
Aerated fluid can accompany heavy effort/noise; this narrow notice directs the
identified condition to WSM 211-00 rather than replacing steering components.
That workshop procedure was not acquired. No universal flush prescription.

## gm-18-na-161-v4

[GM 18-NA-161](https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/tsbs/2020/MC-10181672-9999.pdf),
September 2020, version 4, seven-page text inspected. Page 1 lists exact model/year
scope; reduced electric assist applies only to identified LD EPS models. Pages
2–4 distinguish cable/connection resistance and require loaded voltage-drop
observations before disturbing cables. Other causes include battery/ground/short
faults. SI and referenced 17-NA-345 remain unacquired; numerical limits and
tightening torques are not admitted as current-service approvals here.

## zf-ehps-noise

[ZF EPHS noise technical article](https://aftermarket.zf.com/br/portal-do-aftermarket/central-de-conhecimento/emissao-de-ruido-de-direcao-eletro-hidraulica/),
complete technical body inspected in Portuguese. Undated page, inspected
2026-10-08. Electric pump drive remains hydraulic assist. The article separates
electrical supply, fluid/aeration, line contact and pump mounting from internal pump
failure; an expensive replacement need not remove the noise. Component-maker
guidance, not an exact vehicle procedure or approved fluid type.

## bosch-eps-architecture

[Bosch EPS systems](https://www.bosch-mobility.com/en/solutions/steering/electric-power-steering-systems/),
operating principle and variants inspected. Undated page, inspected 2026-10-08.
Torque input/control/motor assistance and rack/column variants are architecture
evidence. Product marketing does not establish a diagnostic limit or repair.

## gm-pic5468c-2013

[GM PIC5468C](https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/tsbs/2014/SB-10038752-1726.pdf),
January 2013, full two-page bulletin inspected. Scope: 2006–2013 Impala and
2006–2007 Monte Carlo. Page 1 describes air retention in high routed lines after
hydraulic work; pages 1–2 require application-specific bleed and post-repair
verification. This is historical mechanism evidence, not an instruction to loosen
another car's brake lines or a current approved service procedure.

## honda-gl1800-11v567

[Honda GL1800/A stop-sale notice](https://static.nhtsa.gov/odi/rcl/2011/RCORRD-11V567-8637.pdf),
2011-12-02, page 1 mechanism and page 2 service-boundary text inspected. Scope:
specified 2001–2012 GL1800/A motorcycles/VIN ranges. Blocked compensation can
retain rear-brake pressure and generate heat. This motorcycle recall does not
identify a car's fault or supply a car clearance specification.

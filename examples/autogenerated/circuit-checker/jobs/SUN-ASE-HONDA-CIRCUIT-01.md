## 4. Separate topology, electrical behavior and evidence

Implement explicit records for circuit identity, components/terminals, operating state, component laws, instrument configuration, observations, fault hypotheses, access operations and source authority.

Record ignition state, switch command, lockout/enable and connector connection state separately. Model unplugging a connector as an actual circuit change. Idle, UP and DOWN behavior must follow the inspected circuit, not a universal assumption about motor leads.

Distinguish source-verified facts, user-reported observations, assumptions, synthetic fixtures and computed results. Store measurement endpoints/reference, instrument/mode, command state, load condition and uncertainty. Preserve corrections and contradictions without silently overwriting the original report. Unknown is not ground, a failed measurement, or proof of a fault.

A source junction and an unconnected crossing must be distinguishable. Reject duplicate identities, dangling references and inconsistent connector mappings. Do not infer electrical behavior solely from wire color or graph reachability.

## 5. Add bounded electrical and instrument calculations

Retain ideal connectivity and path witnesses. Add a small DC solver for declared resistances, independent sources and switched connections, using exact rational arithmetic for this bounded slice. Document the incidence/sign convention, conservation equations, units and component laws. Check numerical results against independent analytic examples.

Distinguish a unique solution, underdetermined/floating potentials, inconsistent constraints and unsupported behavior. Do not assign a ground reference silently or replace an open circuit with an arbitrary large resistance. Numerical reduction must preserve the mapping back to physical fault locations.

Demonstrate high-resistance feed and return faults, polarity reversal, unloaded versus loaded readings and instrument loading. An ideal path alone must not establish adequate motor current. A simplified winding model is an explicitly declared electrical approximation, not proof that the motor can lift the window or that the regulator is free.

Treat a passive voltage reading, a test-light indication and an active Power Probe operation as different observation/action types. Exact probe model, mode and electrical characteristics remain unknown unless sourced. Unknown characteristics must prevent unsupported numerical predictions; do not assign a generic test-light current or DMM impedance to the user's probe. Synthetic instrument characteristics must be labeled as such.

Keep motor drive and position-sensor supply/return/pulse terminals in separate capability classes. Unsupported active powering or loading of a sensor terminal must be refused by the action contract. This job adds no hardware-control interface and performs no physical energization. Sensor timing, semiconductor transients and full motor mechanics are outside this solver's scope.

## 6. Implement bounded fault diagnosis with explanations

Extend the inherited fault filter to cover declared opens, high-resistance connections, drive/control faults, unavailable motor and mechanical binding, with separately tested nominal, single-fault and bounded two-fault cases.

Expose the modeled fault universe and enumeration bound. If the search is truncated, the output must say incomplete rather than report a complete diagnosis. Unknown predictions cannot eliminate a hypothesis. No survivors means no fit within the declared model/observations, not that the vehicle report is impossible; a single survivor remains conditional on coverage and assumptions.

Return surviving hypotheses and, for each rejected hypothesis, the observation and path/equation/constraint that contradict it. Distinguish observationally equivalent faults rather than forcing a unique component recommendation. Do not invent probabilities from hypothesis counts.

Provide a validated structured-input command interface for model callers: circuit validation, pin-function lookup, inference and test/access comparison, with stable machine-readable output and concise human-readable explanations. Reject malformed requests and arbitrary executable input. The model must invoke the implementation, not paraphrase a prewritten answer.

## 7. Model access work separately from fault identity

Keep incremental test access, repair access and already-completed disassembly separate. Preserve access dependencies and alternative valid repair routes. Report an operation as unavoidable only when it is required across every represented viable repair route of every surviving hypothesis.

For the synthetic continuation in which only internal door wiring, motor and mechanism faults survive, report shared door-cavity access without pretending to know the failed part. Conversely, if an accessible switch or external connection remains a viable repair, do not declare deeper door access universally necessary.

Compare tests by their possible diagnostic discrimination and additional work, accounting for work already required for repair. Do not charge for removing trim already removed. Use supplied effort units or unknown costs, not invented minutes. Cheap accessible checks are useful when they discriminate, but “always test the fuse first” and “always avoid disassembly” are not universal rules.

Retain alternatives when no test dominates. State the conditions under which a comparison holds. This is bounded decision support, not a claim to an optimal whole-repair policy.

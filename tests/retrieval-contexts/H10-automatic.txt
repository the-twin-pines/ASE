# Hot dragging brake: retained pressure or mechanical restraint?

Source honda-gl1800-11v567, 2011-12-02 page 1. Its specific motorcycle secondary
master-cylinder compensation fault illustrates retained pressure after release;
it does not diagnose a car caliper. Synthetic next test: a qualified technician
uses the exact vehicle pressure-release/localization procedure to distinguish
retained hydraulic force from mechanical drag. Drag relieved with controlled
pressure release shifts the ranking upstream; drag retained shifts it toward
mechanical restraint. Neither outcome alone names the hose or master cylinder.

No hot-wheel casual bleeder test, universal clearance, or transferable recall
procedure. Preserve heat injury/fire risk and halt ordinary driving while severe
drag is unresolved. Post-repair drag/temperature and brake-performance checks are
planned under exact service instructions; result NOT_RUN.

# One direction is harder than the other

Synthetic hydraulic trace; source trw-1250-2002, hard-steering chart and test 7.
Sequence: record direction and position, then separate supply, gear and mechanical
load. Hypotheses: direction-dependent gear leakage versus linkage binding.
Discriminator: qualified exact-procedure leakage comparison in both directions,
paired with mechanical localization. Unequal leakage raises gear suspicion;
localized binding shifts attention to mechanics. Neither the symptom nor one
pressure reading proves the gear failed. No owner hose-disconnection test.

Planned verification: repeat both directions after approved repair, retaining
centering, safe travel and leak checks. No repair or physical result: NOT_RUN.

# Caster and camber require valid measurements

Sources: a4-measurement-note and a4-front-end-note, pinned historical reviews.
Preserve RAW readings separately from reference corrections and DERIVED results.
Confirm tire equivalence/pressure, mechanical condition, exact ride-height datum,
four wheel-pad elevations and the instrument's own actual road-wheel sweep.
One bubble level or a steering-wheel turn/nominal ratio cannot establish those
conditions. Constant gauge-zero cancellation does not prove physical floor slope
cancels. Check arithmetic, transcription, geometry, vehicle plausibility and
independent consistency. SUSPECT, INVALID and UNKNOWN stop adjustment advice.

The existing Dakota observations and unmeasured cam-Jacobian remain in notes/.
Passing arithmetic tests does not establish accepted truck alignment or a verified
torque. Do not diagnose a ball joint solely from 12-and-6 tire movement.

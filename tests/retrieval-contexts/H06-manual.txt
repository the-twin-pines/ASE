# Poor return to center or directional pull

Synthetic trace; sources trw-1250-2002 non-recovery/directional-pull charts and
a4-measurement-note. Sequence: separate braking/acceleration/steady-load behavior,
inspect tires, then validate alignment setup. Hypotheses: tire/alignment/load bias
versus friction/assist imbalance. Discriminator: an exact-procedure tire comparison
or localized friction test after measurement validity. A changed pull with an
approved tire comparison moves tire influence up; retained localized resistance
moves binding up. Neither poor return nor a caster display alone proves an assist
fault. No eccentric adjustment from suspect measurements.

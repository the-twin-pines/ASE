# Long pedal after hydraulic work

Scope: historical GM PIC5468C January 2013, listed Impala/Monte Carlo only.
The bulletin distinguishes trapped air in high routed lines after disconnecting
hydraulics. A synthetic discriminator compares firmness, pump-up and steady-force
hold with the repair history before selecting an exact bleed or pressure-holding
procedure. Pump-up alone does not prove the master cylinder failed: clearance/
knock-back and compressibility remain competing mechanisms. Do not treat a firm
pedal as proof that all circuits are safe.

The bulletin supplies an application-specific post-repair pedal and road-test
sequence. No universal bleed order, line-loosening procedure or fluid approval

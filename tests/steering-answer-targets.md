# Six retrieval target answers, not model outputs

1. Parking effort improves with engine speed: identify assist architecture,
   characterize drive/fluid/load/temperature, then separate supply from demand with
   qualified exact-procedure tests. RPM alone does not select a pump.
2. Foam/noise on E450: confirm build and condition against SSM 47522, then obtain
   its WSM. No universal flush or replacement rule; no casual hose pressure test.
3. One direction hard: compare direction/position, separate leakage from binding.
   One pressure reading does not condemn the gear. Exact conditions control testing.
4. EPS warning/radio blanking: retain codes; measure loaded supply/ground before
   disturbing connections. Resting voltage is insufficient; communication, input,
   motor/control remain competing causes.
5. Cyclic binding: localize column/shaft versus wheel-end/linkage under exact
   airbag/centering procedures. Shake position alone does not prove a ball joint.
6. Return/pull: separate tire/load/alignment/drag from friction/assist. Validate
   pad heights and actual instrument sweep. SUSPECT/INVALID/UNKNOWN stop adjustment.

Counterexamples: EPS-only car gets no reservoir/pump prescription; 12-and-6 shake
needs actual relative-motion observation; pressure testing is not a casual owner
task. These authored oracles are not evidence of model compliance. Tests are excluded.
